Key — What Each Digit Means
Digit 1 — Document Type
  • 1 — Prime agreements
  • 2 — Conditions or scope of the agreements
  • 3 — Bonds or qualifications
  • 4 — Agreements between prime and sub-contractors or sub-consultants
  • 5 — Guides
  • 6 — Reserved for future use
  • 7 — Bid documents and construction forms
  • 8 — Forms or documents specific to the architect
Digit 2 — Delivery Method
  • 0–2 — Conventional delivery
  • 3 — CM as adviser or CM as constructor
  • 4 — Design-build
  • 5 — Interior only
  • 6 — International
  • 7 — Program management
  • 8 — Reserved for future use
  • 9 — Integrated project delivery
Digit 3 — Sequence
  • A single digit, 1–9.
  • Tells similar documents apart within the same type and delivery method.
  • Not itself meaningful — it's a tiebreaker, not a category.
